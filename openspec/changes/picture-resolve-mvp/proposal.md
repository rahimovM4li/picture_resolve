## Why

Picture Resolve needs a German business-facing film production site that uses its own work to demonstrate craft. The existing broad agency presentation gives the two primary services little room to answer the commissioning questions marketing and HR teams bring to a first visit.

## What Changes

- Build service destinations for commissioning an Imagefilm or Recruitingfilm, a curated portfolio, a studio introduction, regional content, legal pages and an inquiry preview.
- Present sourced work with locally optimized images, deliberate visual transitions and a reduced-motion experience.
- Provide a local build, route-specific search metadata, tested user journeys and an editorial handoff for launch decisions.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `brand-design-system` — Give Picture Resolve a recognisable editorial language grounded in its existing handwritten identity.
- `responsive-navigation` — Allow visitors to move between services, work, studio information and inquiry from every viewport.
- `homepage` — Introduce the studio through authentic material and connect business visitors with relevant services and work.
- `imagefilm-service` — Help communications and marketing teams evaluate an image film from first idea through delivery.
- `recruitingfilm-service` — Help HR teams plan a credible employer film built around people and everyday work.
- `portfolio-overview` — Let potential clients discover verified Picture Resolve productions and reach each available project.
- `project-detail` — Present each verified film with sufficient context and clearly identified viewing options.
- `about-studio` — Explain the studio’s approach using verified historical information and clearly framed editorial copy.
- `contact-inquiry` — Enable business visitors to validate a project brief and understand exactly where their information goes.
- `location-page` — Describe genuine regional relevance and local production planning without generating ungrounded city pages.
- `seo-metadata` — Make the available German pages discoverable through consistent page metadata and safe preview settings.
- `asset-optimization` — Deliver the studio’s actual creative work in formats and dimensions appropriate to the viewing context.
- `motion-design` — Use deliberate, film-led motion to reveal composition and guide attention without taking over navigation.
- `accessibility` — Support people who navigate by keyboard, use assistive technology, zoom the page or prefer reduced motion.
- `performance` — Keep the showroom responsive on mobile networks and make media and interaction costs measurable.
- `content-architecture` — Provide a typed editorial source that can later be populated by a CMS while keeping MVP data local.

### Modified Capabilities

This greenfield repository has no existing capability specifications.

## Impact

The static artifact can be served locally or by a file host. The live domain, infrastructure, legacy redirects and outbound contact delivery remain outside this implementation. CMS functionality, authentication and data storage are outside MVP v0.1.
