## Purpose

Introduce the studio through authentic material and connect business visitors with relevant services and work.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: From cinematic introduction to inquiry

The homepage SHALL identify the business in German, offer both primary services, genuine project links, working method, credible evidence, contact action and legal navigation.

#### Scenario: Exploring the homepage

- **WHEN** a visitor scrolls from the introduction to the end
- **THEN** they can inspect work, understand the production steps and reach a project inquiry.

#### Scenario: Exploring by keyboard on a phone

- **WHEN** a visitor navigates the compact layout
- **THEN** links expose descriptive names and match the visual reading order.

#### Scenario: Missing showreel

- **WHEN** a playable public reel is unavailable
- **THEN** the hero displays authentic poster material and any offered action opens an available destination.
