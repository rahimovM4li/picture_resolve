## Purpose

Help HR teams plan a credible employer film built around people and everyday work.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A recruiting-specific decision path

The Recruitingfilm page SHALL explain employee voices, work context, planning and candidate touchpoints, and provide a project inquiry.

#### Scenario: Opening the recruiting service

- **WHEN** an HR visitor lands on /leistungen/recruitingfilme
- **THEN** they can understand the distinct production approach and find answers to recruiting-specific questions.

#### Scenario: Exploring on a narrow screen

- **WHEN** a visitor follows links or reads process details with the keyboard
- **THEN** labels and focus remain visible in document order.

#### Scenario: Unavailable recruiting case

- **WHEN** no approved recruiting reference is available
- **THEN** the page describes possible uses and directs to accurately classified studio work.
