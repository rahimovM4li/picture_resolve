## Purpose

Support people who navigate by keyboard, use assistive technology, zoom the page or prefer reduced motion.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Operable, perceivable journeys

Pages SHALL use semantic landmarks, labelled controls, useful alternative text, visible keyboard focus and readable contrast across supported screen sizes.

#### Scenario: Navigating without a pointer

- **WHEN** a visitor tabs through navigation, work and inquiry
- **THEN** every meaningful action can be reached, identified, activated and left.

#### Scenario: Reflow and zoom

- **WHEN** a visitor reads on a narrow viewport or increases text size
- **THEN** critical content remains reachable without clipping or overlapping controls.

#### Scenario: Recovering from an interaction error

- **WHEN** a visitor makes a form input error or requests reduced motion
- **THEN** feedback is perceivable and essential content remains available irrespective of animation.
