## Purpose

Explain the studio’s approach using verified historical information and clearly framed editorial copy.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Studio introduction and working philosophy

The studio page SHALL give a German introduction to the people or craft behind the work, the production approach and an inquiry link.

#### Scenario: Learning about the studio

- **WHEN** a visitor opens /ueber-uns
- **THEN** they can read the approach and follow a clear next step.

#### Scenario: Reading with assistive technology on mobile

- **WHEN** a visitor moves through headings and images
- **THEN** the story has a logical sequence and meaningful portraits or stills have text alternatives.

#### Scenario: Ambiguous team membership

- **WHEN** the source establishes only a historical credit
- **THEN** the text avoids presenting it as a current role.
