## Purpose

Describe genuine regional relevance and local production planning without generating ungrounded city pages.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Fact-grounded regional landing page

The location template SHALL render individually researched local copy, service links, suitable work or regional evidence, its own metadata and inquiry action.

#### Scenario: Researching Düsseldorf

- **WHEN** a visitor opens an available Düsseldorf area page
- **THEN** the text distinguishes the service market from the postal address and links to relevant services.

#### Scenario: Mobile regional inquiry

- **WHEN** a visitor explores the page at phone width
- **THEN** headings, evidence and inquiry links appear in a readable order.

#### Scenario: Unknown or unsupported city

- **WHEN** a user requests a city without approved local content
- **THEN** the route returns a real 404 without generating a generic landing page.
